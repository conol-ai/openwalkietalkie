OpenWalkieTalkie 0.11.1 fixes lost words when you pause while holding PTT with Apple on-device speech recognition.

## Keep the whole message

Previously, speaking a sentence, pausing, and continuing could replace the earlier sentence with the latest phrase. Releasing PTT then sent only the last part of the message.

The Mac service now retains completed phrases for the entire button hold. Both the live preview and the final transcript include the earlier words. Recognition corrections still replace the current phrase, and intentionally repeated phrases are preserved without duplicating callbacks.

## Update

Choose **Check for Updates**, or install the signed, notarized **0.11.1** Mac app for Apple Silicon or Intel. macOS 13 or later is required. If the service still reports an older version, choose **Service → Restart**.

This is a Mac app and service patch. Firmware remains **0.11.0**; no firmware or bootloader update is required for this fix. Settings and Bluetooth pairing are preserved. The release also includes the existing 0.11.0 board-specific firmware and factory packages; factory images are for manufacturing and overwrite settings and pairing.

## Validation

The real Apple on-device recognizer preserved three synthesized English sentences separated by three-second pauses, in both the live preview and the final result. Regression tests cover multiple pauses, partial corrections, repeated phrases, final corrections, cumulative results and empty finals. Audio and PTT integration tests and Clippy passed locally. The release pipeline also checks the Mac workspace and all three firmware targets before signing and notarizing both Mac packages.
