OpenWalkieTalkie 0.15.0 brings the companion app to Omarchy, and makes the deck a dictation microphone there:
- the companion app runs on Omarchy (Linux), in its app launcher;
- PTT on the Omarchy face dictates at your cursor;
- the wheel on the Omarchy face follows the window rows you see;
- the service keeps all its files in one folder on Linux.

The deck's firmware is unchanged: it stays at 0.14.0.

## The companion app on Omarchy

The app the Mac and Windows have now runs on Omarchy (Hyprland), as **OpenWalkieTalkie** in the app launcher, with the same five pages: **Deck**, **Faces & Keys**, **Integrations**, **Voice** and **Service**. It finds the service by itself. On Linux:

- **Service › Start / Stop at login** runs the service's systemd user unit (`deckd.service`), and a restart from the window is the unit's.
- **Open at login** is an XDG autostart entry, which Omarchy's session starts.
- There is no tray icon (that is the desktop bar's job) and no in-app update: the app is built from source, so updating is `git pull`, build, and `install.sh` again.

**Faces & Keys** also fits a tiled window narrower than the app's design (a quarter of the screen, say): the face strip wraps onto a second row and the key editor goes under the key grid.

To install it, build it beside the service and give both to `install.sh`; the [Omarchy guide](https://openwalkietalkie.org/docs/omarchy/) has the steps.

## Dictation on the Omarchy face

On the Omarchy face, PTT works like Superwhisper's own push-to-talk: hold to talk, release, and the words are typed at the cursor of whatever window has the keyboard, with no Enter. CANCEL while you hold aborts the recording, and nothing is typed. When Superwhisper is installed it does the recognition, in its own mode, language and vocabulary; otherwise the local Whisper model does.

Behind it, the Omarchy bridge puts a **Dictate** entry on the roster and makes it the deck's active channel while the deck shows the Omarchy face, putting your channel back when you leave. Selecting **Dictate** on the Agents face and pressing PTT dictates the same way.

## The wheel follows the rows

On the Omarchy face, the wheel now moves through the window rows in the order the face lists them, the way it moves through the deck's other lists, and stops at the first and last row. Before, it followed Hyprland's own window order, so a turn could jump past the row below.

## One folder on Linux

On Linux the service now keeps its config, state, logs, caches and socket in one folder, `~/.local/share/openwalkietalkie` (or `$XDG_DATA_HOME/openwalkietalkie`), as one folder holds them on a Mac. The Omarchy service already ran there, so an Omarchy install has nothing to move, and `deckd` commands no longer need the `DECKD_DATA_DIR=…` prefix. A `deckd` run by hand on Linux with the earlier XDG layout (config in `~/.config`, socket under `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`) looks in the new folder now; move its files there, or set `DECKD_DATA_DIR`.

## Update

On a Mac, choose **Check for Updates…**, or install the signed, notarized **0.15.0** app for Apple Silicon or Intel (macOS 13 or later). On Windows, download the ZIP from the release. On Omarchy, pull, build the service and the app, and run `install.sh` with both, as the [Omarchy guide](https://openwalkietalkie.org/docs/omarchy/) shows. The deck needs no firmware update for this release.
