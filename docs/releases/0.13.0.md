OpenWalkieTalkie 0.13.0 brings English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Japanese to both the Mac app and the deck. Choose the language on either one; the setting is saved and synchronized when they connect.

## Four languages, one setting

On the Mac, open **Service → Language**. On the deck, open **Settings → Display → Language**; scroll down in Display to reach the row. The choices always show their own names: **English**, **简体中文**, **繁體中文** and **日本語**.

The interface changes immediately, including the roster, conversation controls, settings, setup, permissions, notifications, screen lock, tutorial, games and Timer. Chats, tool output, custom names, file paths and physical key labels keep their original text. Speech recognition and spoken replies keep their configured engines and languages.

Both the app and the deck remember the choice across restarts. A language selected on the deck while disconnected is saved locally and reconciled with the app on reconnect. The higher saved language revision wins; the Mac app wins a tie. An old saved strip or settings restore does not overwrite a newer language choice. The Mac setting can also be changed before a deck is connected.

## More precise timing, audible finishes

The stopwatch now shows hundredths of a second. Countdown and Pomodoro finishes ring through the deck's speaker at its configured volume: quick beeps for a countdown, a rising chime for the end of focus and a falling chime for the end of a break.

An alarm stops after 30 seconds, or as soon as you press a button, turn the wheel or touch the screen, including the input that wakes it. Restarting or resetting a timer, or starting the next Pomodoro phase, also stops it. The timers keep running on other pages and while the screen sleeps.

## A larger roster and fewer stale prompts

- The Agents roster is a carousel with a large selected-session card, neighboring previews and a project pager. Rev A and Rev B support up to 255 sessions when their PSRAM session table is available; questions receive priority.
- Claude permission requests already decided elsewhere stay off the deck, and completed or interrupted requests clear their stale cards. Codex returns to working after automatic approval when no approval of its own is pending.
- The Mac app recovers a stale service registration left by a moved or replaced app. Service diagnostics recognize app-registered launchd jobs and detect a daemon running from another app bundle.
- Firmware code now runs from NOR on every board. Rev A no longer copies its code into PSRAM; PSRAM remains available for session data.

## Update

Choose **Check for Updates**, or install the signed, notarized **0.13.0** Mac app for Apple Silicon or Intel. macOS 13 or later is required. If the service still reports an older version, choose **Service → Restart**.

Install firmware **0.13.0** for the deck's translated interface and two-way language synchronization. With the deck connected by USB, open **Service → Updates → Check firmware**. Use the matching 0.13.0 app and firmware. Settings and Bluetooth pairing are preserved by the regular update; factory images are for manufacturing and overwrite them.

## Validation

Automated coverage checks all four translation catalogs, placeholder values, preserved host content, language persistence and reconnect conflict handling. Render tests exercise translated device screens and language changes, including the rounded display's safe area. Timer tests cover hundredths, completion sounds, dismissal and the 30-second limit.
