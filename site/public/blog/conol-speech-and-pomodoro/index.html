OpenWalkieTalkie v0.12.0: Conol hears you, plus Pomodoro

November 5, 2026Mac app, service and firmware

When you talk to a Conol chat from the deck, the words should be the ones Conol would have heard if you had typed into it. Version 0.12.0 hands those presses to Conol's own speech model, shows you a recording timeline while you talk, mirrors what Conol is doing right now, and adds a Timer face with Pomodoro.

Conol transcribes its own chats

Hold PTT on a Conol chat or + New chat and the Mac service records the press and passes it to the Conol desktop app. Conol transcribes it with the cloud speech model behind its composer's microphone, and that text is what the deck shows after release and what is sent, including dictated answers to Conol's questions.

The Mac's own recognizer keeps running during the press. If Conol can't answer, fails, or takes longer than 20 seconds, the Mac's transcript is used, so a press is never lost. Other agents and naming the deck stay on your Mac. Only presses addressed to Conol chats leave the Mac, and only to the Conol app you are signed in to; set [voice.stt] companion = false to keep every press local. This needs the updated Conol desktop and web versions; with an older Conol app, nothing changes.

A timeline while you talk

The Mac's live words are not what Conol will hear, so the deck no longer shows them for these presses. Instead it shows RECORDING, a large elapsed time and a timeline: a red playhead walks left to right while the waveform of your voice, measured by the deck's own microphone, fills in behind it. Long presses scroll. After release the deck shows TRANSCRIBING, then Conol's transcript.

See what Conol is doing

A running Conol chat now shows its current public step on the deck, such as "Running a command" with a short preview, both in the roster and in the conversation. It updates in place and clears when the step ends. Full tool inputs and results stay in Conol.

Timer and Pomodoro

Add Timer from the face manager's + gallery for a stopwatch with laps, a countdown with presets, and Pomodoro: 25 minutes of focus and 5-minute breaks, with a 15-minute break after four focus sessions. Each phase waits for Next / Enter. The timers run on the deck itself, keep going on other pages and while the screen sleeps, and wake the screen with a toast when they finish.

For companion developers

Any native companion with its own recognizer can register caps.transcribe: "push", receive agent.transcribe offers, page the 16 kHz WAV with agent.transcribe.audio and answer with agent.transcribe.result. Activity rows use message.summary on agent.reply. The native companion guide and the API reference describe both.

Update

Choose Check for Updates, or install the signed, notarized 0.12.0 Mac app for Apple Silicon or Intel. macOS 13 or later is required. Firmware 0.12.0 is needed for the recording timeline and the Timer face: with the deck on USB, open Service → Updates → Check firmware. Settings and Bluetooth pairing are preserved.
