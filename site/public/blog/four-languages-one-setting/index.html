OpenWalkieTalkie v0.13.0: Four languages, one setting

November 8, 2026Mac app, service and firmware

Version 0.13.0 brings English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Japanese to both the Mac app and the deck. Pick a language on either one; it is saved across restarts and shared when they connect.

Choose the language where you are

On the Mac, open Service → Language. On the deck, open Settings → Display → Language, scrolling down in Display to reach it. The four choices always show their own names: English, 简体中文, 繁體中文 and 日本語.

The interface changes immediately: the roster and conversation controls, setup, settings, permissions, notifications, screen lock, tutorial, games and Timer. Chats, tool output, custom names, file paths and physical key labels retain their original text. Speech recognition and spoken replies keep their configured engines and languages.

Remembered on both sides

A language selected on the disconnected deck is saved locally. Reconnecting reconciles it with the Mac's saved choice. The higher saved language revision wins; the Mac app wins a tie. An old strip or settings restore does not reset a newer language choice. You can also choose the app's language before connecting a deck. Use the matching 0.13.0 app and firmware for two-way synchronization.

Hundredths and audible finishes

The stopwatch now shows hundredths of a second. A finished countdown beeps through the deck's speaker; Pomodoro plays a rising chime at the end of focus and a falling one at the end of a break, at the speaker's configured volume.

An alarm stops after 30 seconds or at the first button press, wheel movement or screen touch, including an input that wakes the deck. Restarting or resetting a timer, or starting the next Pomodoro phase, stops it too. Timers keep running on other pages and while the screen sleeps.

More sessions, fewer stale questions

The Agents roster is now a carousel with a large selected-session card, neighboring previews and project paging. Rev A and Rev B support up to 255 sessions when their PSRAM session table is available, with questions given priority.

Claude permission requests decided elsewhere stay off the deck, and completed or interrupted requests clear stale cards. Codex returns to working after automatic approval when no approval of its own is pending. The Mac app also recovers stale service registrations left by a moved or replaced app, and service diagnostics recognize app-registered launchd jobs.

Firmware code now runs from NOR on every board. Rev A no longer copies its code into PSRAM; PSRAM remains available for session data.

Update

Choose Check for Updates, or install the signed, notarized 0.13.0 Mac app for Apple Silicon or Intel. macOS 13 or later is required. If the service still reports an older version, choose Service → Restart.

With the deck connected by USB, open Service → Updates → Check firmware and install firmware 0.13.0. The regular update preserves settings and Bluetooth pairing. Factory images are for manufacturing and overwrite them.
